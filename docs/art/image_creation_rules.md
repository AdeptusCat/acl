These rules apply to every single-hex and multihex building, in every orientation:
1. Stay inside the footprint. All visible artwork—including roofs, walls, eaves and chimneys—must remain within the assigned hex or combined hexes.
2. Cover every occupied hex’s center. Visible building pixels must cover the exact center of each occupied hex. A three-hex building must cover all three centers.
3. Keep the camera viewpoint fixed. The building turns through the six orientations while the camera’s direction and elevation remain unchanged. Roof slopes, visible walls and chimney perspective must be rendered correctly for each angle, as in your attachment. Simply rotating the finished image is incorrect.
